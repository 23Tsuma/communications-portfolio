# Safari Stories Portfolio

Haki Yetu Charitable Trust is currently hiring for a Communications Associate based in Mombasa. [1, 2]

Job Overview

Position: Communications Associate

Location: Mombasa, Kenya (with field travel as required)

Deadline to Apply: November 22, 2026

Salary Estimate: KES 60,000 – 90,000 per month [1, 2, 3]

Key Responsibilities

Manage digital content production, publication, and tracking across web, print, and social media platforms.

Provide first-line ICT and technical support for organizational equipment and systems.

Translate and adapt communications materials between English and Kiswahili.

Support advocacy campaigns, media briefings, press events, and monitor media coverage.

Maintain media contact lists, photo/video libraries, and apply ethical storytelling guidelines. [1, 2, 3]

Requirements

Education: Bachelor’s degree in Communication, Journalism, Mass Media, Public Relations, Digital Media, Information Technology, or a related field.

Experience: Minimum of 1 year of experience in communications, digital content, or ICT support (internships count).

Skills: Proficiency in graphic design tools (such as Canva or Adobe), video editing, social media management, website management, and basic networking/ICT troubleshooting.

Languages: Strong written and verbal skills in both English and Kiswahili. [1, 2]
